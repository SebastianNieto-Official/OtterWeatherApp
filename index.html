      Information

      Creator

      AET 330T student project by SebastianNieto-Official on GitHub.

      Weather data source

      All current and forecast weather data comes from Open-Meteo, used under its CC BY 4.0 license and free, non-commercial tier.

      Recommendation methods and sources

      • Outfit categories are based on feels-like (apparent) temperature bands. No single authoritative source defines clothing-by-temperature bands, so these cutoffs (Scorching 90°F+, Warm 70-89°F, Mild 48-69°F, Cold 33-47°F, Freezing 32°F and below) are a Developer design choice.
      • Sunscreen and hat reminders follow the EPA UV Index Scale (UV 3 and above).
      • The hydration reminder follows NWS heat index guidance (feels-like 80°F and above).
      • Cold-weather layering is informed by NWS wind chill guidance.
      • The footwear rule (open-toe only in warm, dry, low-wind conditions) and the rain/poncho thresholds are a Developer design choice.

      Privacy

      • Device location is requested only when you tap "Use my location" - never automatically.
      • Only your most recent location (name and coordinates) is stored on this device, in your browser's local storage. Checklist ticks, the pause-animation and day/night settings, and outfit/wording history are never stored.
      • This app sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking of any kind.
      • Using this app sends your coordinates or place name to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast. The site host, GitHub Pages, receives the same ordinary request data (such as your IP address) that any web host receives.
